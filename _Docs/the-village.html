The organising idea

Not a centre. A village.

The village my grandparents lived in is where I learned the most — by the river, among the older children, with the whole place looking out for me. This is that village, rebuilt: a place a child is of, not just sent to.

What the village grows in every child: courage, curiosity, belonging.

1The river & the land. Nature is the classroom, not the recess. Climbing, digging, watching, small real risks.
2The hearths. The rooms a child belongs to — a secure base. Belonging starts with a place that's yours.
3The gathering place. Families and community. Parents aren't drop-offs; they're villagers — events, conversations, shared rituals.
4The story tree. Educators as guides, not instructors. Songs, two languages, and the older children teaching the younger.
5The workshop. Where children make, test, and document — inquiry, loose parts, the thinking wall. EXPLORE lives here.
6The gate. Safety, ratios, and policies tend the edge so children can roam freely inside. The gate is also the welcome — the tour.
7The bridge. Out to school and the wider world — and a village big enough to hold two worlds at once. Ready for life.
